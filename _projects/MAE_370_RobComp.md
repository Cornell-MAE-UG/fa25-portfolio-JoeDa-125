For my mechatronics class, the our final project was to build a block collecting robot to compete in a competition sponsored by ASML, a world leading semiconductors company. To accomplish the task of collecting more blocks than the other teams' robots, we used a combination of circuits, sensor, and clever arduino code to make a robot that performed well at competition.
