For this project, my team was assigned to take 
apart a weed wacker and analyze its fluid 
mechanical components. For our device, we 
analyzed 3 main components: the carburetor, 
the engine, and the throttle. The carburetor's 
job is to mix air and fuel by creating a 
pressure drop using a venturi that will suck 
up fuel out of its resevior. This air fuel 
mixture gets fed to the engine which combusts 
the mixture to produce rotation. This rotation 
eventually ends up turning the spindle at the 
end of the device via a flexible drive shaft. 
The speed of the engine is controlled through 
a throttle, which controls the airflow into 
the carburetor by varying the angle of a 
butterfly valve.
